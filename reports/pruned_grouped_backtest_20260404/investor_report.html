The Session Turtle Trend x3 is a rule-based, systematic trend-following strategy. It operates on 5-minute bars across a 21-symbol universe spanning crypto, equities, gold, metals, and energy. Positions are entered on Donchian channel breakouts and sized via risk-percentage position sizing at 3.0× notional exposure. The strategy trades both long and short, with multiple overlaid filters to manage regime and drawdown risk.

The strategy runs two asset groups with different channel speeds: fast breakout (10/5) for high-noise commodities and high-beta equities, and slow breakout (20/10) for crypto and mega-cap equities. Crypto assets also trade the Hong Kong session (01:30 UTC) in addition to the New York equity open, providing near-24/7 coverage that is critical to overall returns.

Concentration & Tail Risk

Win Concentration: The top 10 trades by net PnL account for 79.6% of total net profit. The top 10 winning trades account for 58.9% of gross wins. The strategy is highly dependent on a small number of large-winner trades — a characteristic of trend-following that is expected, but means dry spells between major trends can feel painful.
Q1 2026 Recency Concentration: Q1 2026 alone generated +$34,369 (+176% return on Jan 1 equity), representing roughly 65% of total 4-year profit. The “exclude Q1 2026” scenario brings CAGR to 114.6% — still strong, but investors should understand that the headline 162% is heavily influenced by the most recent quarter.
2026 Win Rate: Q1 2026 shows a 70.4% win rate on 27 trades, well above the 41.4% full-period average. This is either an exceptionally good quarter or a sign that the recent market regime is unusually favourable for breakout strategies. Live performance should be monitored closely.
HK Session is Load-Bearing: Removing the Hong Kong crypto session drops CAGR from 162% to 56% and raises max drawdown to 37%. The dual-session design (HK + NY) for crypto and PAXG (tokenized gold) is structural to the strategy's return profile and must be preserved in live trading.
