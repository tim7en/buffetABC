2. The Hypothesis

The standard Donchian channel implementation uses a single period across all assets. This works reasonably across a broad universe but creates a structural mismatch: high-volatility assets (crypto, single-stock tech) generate breakout signals too slowly on a 20-bar channel, while low-volatility assets (ETFs, large-cap equity indices) produce false breakouts at a 10-bar level due to noise.

The experiment: Split the 27-symbol universe into two groups based on observed annualised volatility. Group A (17 volatile assets) keeps the existing fast 10-bar entry / 5-bar exit channel. Group B (10 smoother assets) switches to a slower 20-bar entry / 10-bar exit channel. All other strategy parameters — position sizing, overlays, drawdown governor, capital — remain identical.

3. Group Definitions

GROUP A  Fast Channel — 10-bar entry / 5-bar exit

High-volatility assets: crypto, precious metals, energy, and high-beta equities. Annualised vol range: 33%–150%. Fast channel captures explosive moves quickly and exits before large reversals consume gains.

BTC-USDETH-USDSOL-USD PAXG-USDSLVXPD-USD PPLTBRENTNATGAS-USD COINCRCLHOOD INTCMETAMSTR PLTRTSLA

GROUP B  Slow Channel — 20-bar entry / 10-bar exit

Lower-volatility assets: macro ETFs, large-cap equity, and diversified indices. Annualised vol range: 16%–136%. Slower channel filters out intraday noise and requires a more sustained breakout before committing capital.

COPPER-USDAMZN GOOGLNVDA EWJEWY QQQSPY AAPLTSM

8. Key Findings & Next Steps

✔ Validated
Adaptive channel periods by volatility group is a significant structural improvement. DUAL_PERIOD = CAGR 130% vs BASELINE 31%, same universe.
⚠ Observation
Group B assets are borderline at best. AAPL, AMZN, EWJ, and QQQ are consistent detractors even on their "corrected" 20/10 channel.
🔍 Next Steps
(1) Remove AAPL and AMZN from the universe.
(2) Walk-forward validate DUAL_PERIOD on 2024–2026 hold-out.
(3) Consider Group A/B re-classification quarterly.

Detailed Recommendations

  • Remove AAPL — worst Group B performer in both DUAL_PERIOD and GROUP_B_ONLY runs. PF 0.13 on DUAL_PERIOD suggests no edge exists.
  • Remove or reduce AMZN — PF 0.02 in DUAL_PERIOD (−$594). Near-zero short history limits shorting edge.
  • Monitor ETH-USD — single largest Group A detractor (−$1,001, PF 0.56). May benefit from tighter exits or a sentiment veto during bear cycles.
  • Monitor NATGAS-USD — strong long-side history (+$158) offset by extreme short-side losses (−$962). Consider long-only or size reduction.
  • Run walk-forward validation — restrict training data to 2022–2023 and validate on 2024–2026 before adjusting production config.
  • Quarterly group re-classification — volatility regimes shift. Review vol profiles every quarter and re-assign symbols between A/B groups if needed.
